I am a PhD researcher in Generative AI, specializing in Large Language Models (LLMs), and an AI Engineer focused on turning research into practical products.

Over the past 6+ years, I've worked across NLP, machine learning, deep learning, and generative AI. Earlier in my career, I worked as a Software Engineer specializing in .NET/C#, across several software companies. More recently, I've focused on building LLM-powered applications and RAG-based systems, and I'm currently expanding into agentic AI through hands-on study and an upcoming certification.

I'm driven by closing that gap well — building systems that are practical, accessible, and genuinely useful, not just technically impressive.
